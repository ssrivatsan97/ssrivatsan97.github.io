### **Education** ###
1. B.Tech. in Electrical Engineering<br/>
Indian Institute of Technology Bombay (Mumbai, India)
2. PhD in Electrical Engineering<br/>
Stanford University (California, USA)

My current research is on secure distributed systems and cryptography with applications to blockchain protocols.

I'm currently a postdoctoral scholar at UC Berkeley working with [Prof. Sanjam Garg](https://people.eecs.berkeley.edu/~sanjamg/). Before that, I was a senior researcher at [Babylon](https://babylonlabs.io/).
I obtained my PhD from Stanford University advised by [Prof. David Tse](https://tselab.stanford.edu/). I have previously done internships with [Mysten Labs](https://mystenlabs.com/), [Protocol Labs](https://protocol.ai/), and [Subspace Labs](https://subspace.network/).

Please look at my [projects](/projects) to know about my research and publications.
